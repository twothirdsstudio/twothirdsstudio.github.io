How it works

One photo at a time. Four directions, nothing else.

Swipe right

Keep.

Swipe left

Set aside. The photo goes into the “Zum Löschen” (To Delete) album.

Swipe up

Move into one of your folders.

Swipe down

Move into your last-used folder.

Changed your mind? Tap “Back” to undo any decision.

Nothing gets lost

Ordnerei never deletes anything itself. Everything you set aside goes into the “Zum Löschen” (To Delete) album; you take the final step in the Photos app.

Your folders, your order

Create your own folders, such as Holidays, Family or Screenshots. They show up as albums right in the Photos app.

Sort your way

Filter by type, album, year, month or day. Pick a capture date in the calendar, and a daily goal keeps you going.

Private by design.

Frequently asked questions

Does Ordnerei delete my photos?

No. Ordnerei only puts what you set aside into the “Zum Löschen” (To Delete) album. Deleting is up to you, in the Photos app.

Do I need an account?

No. Ordnerei works without an account and uploads nothing.

Where do I find my folders?

As albums right in the Photos app.
