Ordnerei

Sort your photos, rediscover your memories

Coming soon to the App Store

Swipe through your library, from your oldest photos to today. Move pictures into your own folders with a single swipe, keep what matters and set aside what can go. Along the way you’ll run into moments you had long forgotten. Ordnerei never deletes anything itself: everything you set aside goes into the album “Zum Löschen” (To Delete), and you take the final step in the Photos app.

  • Swipe left to set aside, right to keep; up to choose a folder, down to use the last folder.
  • Your own folders as albums in the Photos app.
  • Filter photos and videos by media type, album or date.
  • Explore capture dates in the calendar and set a daily goal.

How we work

No account needed

Open the app and get started. No sign-up, no email address.

No ads, no tracking

No ad networks, no analytics tools, no snooping.

Everything stays on your iPhone

We receive no photos or app data. Content is passed on only when you choose to share or copy it. Apple manages iCloud and device backups.
