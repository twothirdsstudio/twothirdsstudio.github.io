Frequently asked questions

Does Ordnerei delete my photos?

No. Ordnerei only adds photos and videos you set aside to the album “Zum Löschen” (To Delete). To delete them: open Photos › Albums › “Zum Löschen” › Select › select all › Delete. Choose deletion from the library, rather than just removing items from the album. Deleted items remain in Recently Deleted for 30 days unless you permanently delete them there sooner.

Ordnerei doesn’t show all my photos.

You may have allowed access to selected photos only. Change this in Settings › Apps › Ordnerei › Photos › Full Access (on older iOS versions: Settings › Ordnerei › Photos). Also check your filters: favourites are skipped by default.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone: sorting progress and daily statistics are stored in local files; settings and your last position are stored in local preferences. Ordnerei has no app-specific iCloud sync. Depending on your settings, this data may be included in an iPhone backup with Apple or on your computer. Albums belong to your Photos library and may sync through iCloud Photos.

What happens if I delete Ordnerei?

Deleting the app removes its local settings and saved progress. Your photos and albums in Photos remain. After reinstalling, Ordnerei can read existing albums again; keep-only progress is lost unless you restore a suitable backup. Offloading the app keeps its data.

Can I get a reminder for my daily goal?

Yes. Turn on reminders in Ordnerei’s settings and allow notifications. You choose the time. Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone for days when your daily goal is still open.

Why does a photo or video need internet access?

If the original is only in iCloud, Ordnerei requests it through Apple’s Photos framework. iOS handles the download. You can copy photos and share videos using the iOS share sheet. Your chosen services’ settings and privacy policies apply; Apple’s Handoff may make copied content available on other devices.
