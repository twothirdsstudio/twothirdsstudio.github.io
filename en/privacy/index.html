2. At a glance

Ordnerei processes your photos and sorting progress on your iPhone. We receive no personal data from the app. There are no ads, analytics or tracking. The website uses no cookies or trackers. Necessary access data processed by GitHub Pages, email contact and Apple or sharing features you use are explained below.

3. The Ordnerei app

The “Privacy”, “Support” and “Imprint” links open the website in Safari, while “Contact” opens your email app; the app itself does not transmit anything when opening these links.

Photo library access (read and write) is used to display photos and videos, create albums and organise media within them. Ordnerei does not delete photos or videos from your library. Optional notification permission is used for local daily-goal reminders. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or location access.

Local storage contains media identifiers for kept and filed items, daily statistics, folder names and album identifiers, recently used folders, sorting plan, filters, daily goal, reminder time, last position and timing information for a possible rating prompt. The app uses files in its Documents directory and UserDefaults, iOS’s local preferences storage. Video sharing creates temporary files in the app’s temporary directory; the previous export is removed on the next export. iOS may clear temporary files.

The app has no server of its own and no app-specific iCloud or CloudKit sync. Apple may download originals from iCloud through PhotoKit and sync albums through iCloud Photos if you use those services. Depending on your settings, app data may be included in iPhone backups with Apple or on your computer. Copying a photo to the system clipboard may make it available on other Apple devices through Handoff. Sharing a video through the iOS share sheet passes the content to the recipient or service you choose. A rating prompt is displayed through Apple’s StoreKit. Apple’s policies or those of your chosen providers apply to these system services.

We include no third-party SDKs, ad networks, analytics or crash-reporting services. We receive no photos, usage data or identifiers from the app. Local app data is stored until the app is deleted, unless changed or removed earlier. Photos and albums in Photos, and existing backups, are unaffected.

4. Website and hosting

The website is provided through GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary access data, particularly your IP address; according to GitHub, it is logged and stored for security purposes. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is to provide the website securely and free of charge. Data may be transferred to the USA. Where applicable, the transfer relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR) or standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). For more information, see GitHub’s privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. The website itself uses no cookies and does not embed external fonts, content or services.

5. Email contact

If you email us, we process your email address, message and, where provided, your name only to answer your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for contractual or pre-contractual enquiries, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR based on our legitimate interest in answering enquiries. We delete the data once the enquiry is resolved, unless statutory retention duties apply.
